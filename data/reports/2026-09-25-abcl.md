# ABCL (AbCellera): why so strong, 2026-09-25
As of: prices 2026-09-24 close · X corpus through 2026-09-25 · runway record 2026-09-24 · 13F 2026-06-30 (12 weeks)
Regime: Lean risk-off (composite -4.8), duration hostile · long-duration cap 15%

## Answer
ABCL is strong because of one clinical event. On 2026-08-10, **ABCL635**, a single-dose NK3R antibody for menopausal hot flashes, met both Phase 2 primary endpoints. The stock rose 85% in five sessions, is +90% over 3 months, and sits at $12.65, 4% under its 52-week high and 123% above its 200-day. A funded balance sheet, an insider buying cluster and a short base of about 15% of float kept it running. It is not strong on the business: Q2 revenue missed badly and the losses are large. The move now hangs on a binary event, the full-data oral presentation at the **IMS World Congress, 30 Sep to 3 Oct**, a week from now. Verdict: the 24 Sep probe rated it **no runway**, with expected +1.2% over 6 months. For this mandate, don't chase into the readout. At most a small satellite position after the data, on a base near the insiders' $10–11 buys.

## Why
- **The trigger was a trial result, not the earnings print** (`records/ABCL.json`, `2026-09-18-zeta-abcl/probe.md`).
  - Trial: 92 women, one 600 mg subcutaneous dose. Hot flashes fell by 8.8 a day vs 3.5 for placebo at week 4 (p<0.001), an 83% reduction from baseline vs 33% for placebo.
  - Safety: no serious adverse events.
  - The pitch against the approved daily oral pills, Astellas's Veozah and Bayer's Lynkuet: potentially once-monthly dosing, and no liver monitoring.
- **Funding removed the usual biotech worry.** They raised $200M at $9.75 (oversubscribed, about 6–7% dilution), for about $765M of pro forma cash plus about $110M of non-dilutive government funding. That matters in a high-rate tape: ABCL does not need to issue stock into weakness, unlike the "tier 2" names.
- **Insiders bought after the data**, about $1.55M on the open market:
  - CFO Booth: $384K at $10.32
  - Director Hayden: $1.05M at about $10.37–10.64
  - Director Montalbano: $110K at $11.03

  The 24 Sep record says it found no insider trades; its Finviz source missed these buys. The 18 Sep probe and CEOStockWatcher both recorded them (post 2089382267533570307).
- **The Street and flows lean in.** Consensus is Strong Buy (9 analysts, average target $17.13, +35%). Truist has $30; Goldman initiated at Hold $13 on 24 Sep. Institutions: 77 buyers vs 29 sellers. Short interest of about 15% of float with 9.6 days to cover adds squeeze fuel.
- **The allowlist barely covers it.** There are 3 posts ever and no formal pick; TheLongInvest's is an Elliott Wave recap (2086900319867342864). This is not a crowded X trade.

**Why the probe still says no runway:**
- **Delivery failed:** Q2 revenue was $4.05M vs $7.85M expected, and FY26 revenue estimates are about $25M (-66%). Stock comp runs at 77% of revenue, and the underlying burn is about $40M a quarter.
- **Valuation:** EV/sales is 55×, and the stock is up about 270% year to date, so the good news is priced.
- **Competition:** approved rivals already exist. The data runs 4 weeks, there is no head-to-head comparison, and a 92-patient study can't prove commercial superiority.
- **Macro fit:** 1 of 10, a long-duration biotech in a 2.76% real-yield regime.

## What would change it
- **2026-09-30 to 10-03, IMS full data:** durability past week 4 and a clean safety table support a Phase 3 design and a base above $11. Weaker durability or a new safety signal means exit, and don't average down (the 18 Sep kill test).
- **Q4 2026, ABCL575 Phase 1 topline:** a second credible asset turns a one-drug story into a platform story.
- **~2026-11-05, Q3 results:** watch the burn against the $765M cash pile. The Phase 3 cost estimate sets how long that cash lasts.
